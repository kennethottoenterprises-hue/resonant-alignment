Privacy Policy

I am the sole author running this site myself. I collect the smallest amount of information needed to do what you asked, and nothing more.

What I collect, and why

Your email address
Collected when you request the free guide, Resonance, Not Attraction, or subscribe to Sanctuary by email. Used only to send you that guide, the Sanctuary letter if you subscribe, and a small number of related follow-up emails. Handled through Kit (formerly ConvertKit). Every email has an unsubscribe link, and it works immediately.
Your mailing address
Collected only if you subscribe to the printed Sanctuary tier, only to address and mail your letter. It is never sold, rented, shared, or used for anything else.
Payment information
Handled entirely by Stripe. I never see or store your card number, expiry date, or CVC. Stripe's own privacy policy governs that data.
Anything else
This site does not use tracking cookies, ad pixels, or analytics scripts that build a profile of you. Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type) may be kept briefly by GitHub Pages, which hosts this site, under GitHub's own privacy policy — I do not access these for marketing purposes.

What I do not do

I do not sell your information to anyone, for any reason. I do not share it with advertisers. If I ever want to quote something you've written to me — a reply to an email, a story you've shared — I ask first, and I publish it anonymously unless you tell me otherwise.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time what information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Reach me through the contact form on ottoaligned.com and I will handle it personally, usually within a few days.

Refund Policy

The free guide
Costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund.
The book
Purchased through Amazon, and covered by Amazon's own return policy, not mine.
Sanctuary — billing
Monthly, on the day you started, through Stripe. Your card statement will show OTTOALIGNED.COM.
Sanctuary — cancelling
Any time, from your subscription page — enter the email address you subscribed with and Stripe sends you a sign-in link. You will not be charged again, and you will still receive the letter for the month you have already paid for. If you would rather just tell me, email kenneth@ottoaligned.com and I will cancel it that week.
Sanctuary — refunds
If you subscribe and it is not what you hoped for, email me within thirty days of your first charge and I will refund that month. I issue refunds the same day you ask, but banks typically take five to ten business days to put the money back on your card — Stripe will email you the moment it is sent.
Missing print issues
If a printed letter goes missing in the post, tell me and I will send another at no charge.
